## Not books, but the real source for 3D and deep zoom

No book covers 3D fractals or modern deep zoom well; the knowledge is in papers and blogs. Fetch and cite these in step A (titles from memory, confirm each):

- John C. Hart, "Sphere Tracing" (1996); Hart, Sandin and Kauffman, "Ray Tracing Deterministic 3-D Fractals" (1989).
- Scott Draves and Erik Reckase, "The Fractal Flame Algorithm".
- Inigo Quilez's articles (distance estimators, orbit traps, smooth iteration count, soft shadows).
- Mikael Hvidtfeldt Christensen's "Distance Estimated 3D Fractals" series (Syntopia blog).
- K. I. Martin's SuperFractalThing notes on perturbation; Claude Heiland-Allen's writing on deep zoom (mathr.co.uk).
- Ryan Geiss's MilkDrop preset authoring guide; the projectM and Butterchurn repositories.
