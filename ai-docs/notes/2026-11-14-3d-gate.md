# 3D look gate, 2026-11-14 (Step E)

Hypothesis tested: 3D DE fractals come out ugly. Built `fw.py 3d` (scripts/fw_3d.py, scripts/page/fractal3d.frag), own GLSL from the published formulas in references/07-3d.md; no Shadertoy, Fragmentarium or Mandelbulber code.

## Setup

- Formulas in the gate: Mandelbulb p8 (10 it), quaternion Julia c = (-0.2, 0.6, 0.2, 0) (12 it), Menger (5 levels, cube minus crosses). Views: `whole` and `close`. Mandelbox and hybrid are built but left out of the gate (see What failed).
- Baseline: flat light (0.3 ambient + 0.7 Lambert), colour from march step count, lazy camera (60 degree vertical FOV, subject centred).
- Variants: each remedy alone (ao, shadow, fog, trap, dof, aa, camera) and all together. 54 renders at 768x512, 3.44 s total on the RTX 5060 Ti (0.01-0.11 s each; all-on with 16 spp is the slowest).
- Files: ask/fractalwright-3d-gate-2026-11-14/gate/ (renders, recipes, six sheets, gate.json timings, judge.json).

## Verification of the unverified refs/07 item

Quaternion Julia DE `0.5 |z| log|z| / |dz|` (refs/07 3.2, marked unverified): it is the Hubbard-Douady potential bound G/|G'| (G = log|z_n| / 2^n) halved, as in Milnor's distance estimate for quadratic Julia sets; checked here numerically only for the Mandelbulb form (same 0.5 r log r / dr shape). The Mandelbulb DE test marched 400 rays and found 4 of 82,806 step samples inside the set (0.00005, bar 0.01). The quaternion form is not separately tested (gap).

## Judge (artwright-judge, pairwise, blind, both orders)

Baseline vs each variant, 8 pairs x 6 formula-views = 48 pairs, 96 judgements by four fresh subagents; each agent saw a shuffled mix and never both orders of one pair; labels 1/2 only. Recorded with `aw.py compare record`, ranked with `aw.py rank --write` (Bradley-Terry). A split between orders is a tie.

| Remedy vs baseline | Win | Loss | Tie |
|---|---|---|---|
| soft shadows | 6 | 0 | 0 |
| ambient occlusion | 5 | 0 | 1 |
| composed camera | 5 | 0 | 1 |
| all together | 4 | 0 | 2 |
| depth of field | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| anti-aliasing | 1 | 0 | 5 |
| orbit-trap colour | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| fog + depth cue | 0 | 4 | 2 |

BT tops: Mandelbulb whole: all, camera, ao, shadow tied (2.44) over baseline (0.63); quatjulia close: shadow, camera, aa, all, ao tied (2.01) over baseline (0.39). Full strengths in gate/judge.json.

## What each remedy did

- Shadow and AO: the clearest gains; recesses and form read. With the iteration palette the effect is subtle but consistent.
- Composed camera: 40 mm, low angle, left third, slight roll; the judge preferred it 5/6, the Menger whole split.
- AA: 4 spp; invisible at judge size (mostly ties). Keep it on; it costs little.
- DOF: blurred too much detail on the close views (loses there), helps on whole views. Aperture needs to depend on the view.
- Trap colour: mixed; on Menger it turned the faces pastel and lost to the baseline.
- Fog: lost or tied everywhere; washes the subject toward the sky colour at these scene sizes.
- All: never lost, but split on Menger (fog and trap drag it down). The likely best default is shadow + ao + camera + aa, fog and dof off.

## What failed

- Mandelbox at scale -1.5 reads as a plain cube from outside at the chosen views; it needs interior or edge-close cameras. Left out of the gate.
- Hybrid (box fold before each bulb step) grows the set far beyond radius 2; the first framing put the camera inside it. Reframed (distance 7), not judged.
- Judge only the agent's taste; Mark's picks decide. `aw.py verdict validate` reports the items "not found" because the verdicts were run from a scratch work folder with paths relative to the set; rankings were written regardless.
- Not done: path tracing, a stack-vs-previous-stack comparison, meshing for print (separate small test after the gate).
